## Full description (max 4000)
Penny shows one calm number: what is safe to spend until your next payday. Everything else is there when you need it, and every feature is free for everyone. No subscription, no ads, no account needed.

ONE NUMBER YOU CAN TRUST
• Safe to spend: your income, minus bills that are still due, money set aside for goals, and what you have already spent, for your real pay cycle (monthly, twice a month, weekly, every two weeks, or "when money lands").
• A daily amount and a gentle pace line, so you know if you are on track before the month surprises you.

BUDGETS THAT FIT HOW YOU LIVE
• Spending limits with rollover, targets and templates, or zero-based budgeting if you like to give every unit a job.
• Bills and subscriptions with reminders, goals with automatic set-asides, loans with a payoff plan.

FAST TO USE
• Quick add with a calculator keypad and your usual amounts, templates, voice entry and receipt scanning on the phone.
• Optional: suggest transactions from your payment and bank app notifications (Penny never reads SMS).
• Import from spreadsheets and other apps, and export everything any time.

SHARE WITHOUT THE AWKWARDNESS
• Split trips, rent and dinners in groups, with the fewest payments to settle up.
• Track money you lend or borrow, money sent home, and savings circles (chit funds, tandas, susus).
• Share a book with your family through a folder you already sync, protected by a share code.

INSIGHTS THAT EXPLAIN
• Where your money went, trends, net worth, a forecast and a private on-device coach.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data stays on your phone in an encrypted database. Penny works fully offline.
• Free sync between your own devices through a folder you choose (for example with Syncthing), encrypted with a recovery phrase only you hold. No Penny account, no Penny server.
• No ads, no tracking SDKs, no data sale, no loans or credit offers.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Any currency and pay cycle, local number formats (including lakh and crore), 31 languages, right-to-left support, dark mode, large text and screen readers.
• Undo everything, a 30-day Trash, and full history of changes.

Penny is free for everyone, rich or poor. If it helps you, you can leave an optional tip; it unlocks nothing and nobody gets less without it.
